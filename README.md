# 4ease
This is to show the V1 features of 4ease

My understanding of the product

Target user: Students at the University of Benin (UNIBEN) who need to find a place to live off-campus.

Problem: Finding decent, affordable housing is hard for these students, and agents often insert themselves as costly middlemen, overcharging students just to connect them with a landlord.

Main journey: A landlord submits their listing through a simple form. A student comes to the site, browses and filters listings to find ones that fit their budget and location, opens a listing to see the full details and photos, and then contacts the landlord directly, cutting the agent out entirely.

Proposed V1 features (7)

1. Landlord listing submission form - price, location, room type, photos, contact info

2. Manual review/approval step before a submitted listing goes public

3. Public listings page showing all approved listings

4. Search/filter by price and location

5. Listing detail page — full info, all photos, landlord contact info

6. Basic listing data storage (so listings persist and can be browsed/filtered)

7. Simple, readable listing cards on the browse page (photo, price, location, room type at a glance)

NOT NOW
- Student/landlord accounts or login
- In-app messaging
- Payments or rent handling
- Roommate matching / rent splitting
- Automated scam or listing verification
- Ratings and reviews
- Native mobile app
- Multi-university expansion
